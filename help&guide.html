🚀 Getting Started Basics
📝
Creating an Account
Sign up with your email and phone number. Verify your email to activate your account. You'll receive a unique SPIKE ID.
▼

Step-by-step Guide

  1. Open the SPIKE app and tap "Get Started" or "Create Account".
  2. Enter your full name, email address, and create a strong password (min 6 characters).
  3. Enter your phone number with country code (e.g., +234).
  4. You'll receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS. Enter it to verify your phone.
  5. After phone verification, you'll be prompted to enter your email verification code sent to your inbox.
  6. Once verified, you'll be assigned a unique SPIKE ID (e.g., SP-123456). This ID is permanent and cannot be changed.
  7. You're now ready to explore SPIKE!
💡 Tip: Use a strong password and enable "Remember Me" for faster login on trusted devices.
⚠️ Note: Your SPIKE ID is displayed on your profile. Share it with friends to connect and receive coins.

Use your email and password to sign in. Enable "Remember Me" for faster access. You can sign out from your profile.
▼

Signing In

💡 Tip: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password?" link on the login screen to reset it via email.

Your SPIKE ID
Every user gets a permanent SPIKE ID (e.g., SP-123456). It's your unique identity on SPIKE.
▼

Your SPIKE ID is a unique, permanent identifier that you can use to:

  • Connect with friends — share your ID so others can find you.
  • Receive coins — others can send you coins using your ID.
  • Identify yourself — your ID appears on your profile and is visible to others.

How to find your SPIKE ID:

  1. Go to your Profile.
  2. Look for the SPIKE ID section (e.g., SP-123456).
  3. Tap on it to copy it to your clipboard.
💡 Tip: Your SPIKE ID is forever permanent. You cannot change it, so keep it safe.

Email Verification
After signing up, check your email for a verification link. You must verify your email before accessing the feed.
▼

Email verification is required to ensure the security of your account and to prevent spam.

How to verify

  1. After signing up, check the inbox of the email you used to register.
  2. Look for an email from SPIKE with the subject "Verify your email address".
  3. Click the verification link inside the email.
  4. You'll be redirected to SPIKE and your account will be activated.
⚠️ Can't find the email? Check your Spam or Junk folder. Add noreply@spike.mediachat.workers.dev to your contacts.
💡 Tip: If you haven't received the email, you can request a new verification email from the verification page.

Creating Posts
Write your thoughts in the post composer. Add images or videos to make your post more engaging.
▼

How to create a post

  1. On the Feed page, locate the post composer at the top (a text area with "What's happening on SPIKE?").
  2. Type your message. You can use hashtags (e.g., #spike) and mentions (e.g., @username).
  3. Optional: Tap the 📷 Image or 🎥 Video buttons to attach media (max 5MB).
  4. Preview your media (you can remove it before posting).
  5. Tap the "Post" button to publish.
💡 Tip: Use relevant hashtags to increase the reach of your post. Mentioning others notifies them.
⚠️ Note: You can edit or delete your post within 5 minutes of posting. After that, only admins can modify it.

Likes, Comments & Reposts
Show appreciation, join conversations, and share posts to your feed.
▼

Like

Tap the ❤️ button on any post. The count increases. Tap again to unlike.

Comment

Tap the 💬 button to open comments. Type your reply and tap "Post".

  • You can also reply to a specific comment by tapping the reply button.
  • Comments can be deleted by the author or an admin.

Repost

Tap the 🔁 button (or the ⋯ menu → Repost) to share the post on your own feed.

💡 Tip: Reposting is a great way to share interesting content with your followers.

Hashtags & Mentions

▼

Hashtags

Type # followed by a word (e.g., #spike). Hashtags become clickable links that show all posts with that tag.

Mentions

Post Actions (Edit, Delete, Pin, Hide)
Manage your posts via the ⋮ menu. Admins have additional powers.
▼

Accessing the menu

Tap the ⋮ (three dots) on any post you own (or if you're an admin).

Available actions

  • Edit — Change the text content. Only available within 5 minutes of posting.
  • Delete — Remove the post permanently. Cannot be undone.
  • Pin — Pin the post to the top of the feed (admin only).
  • Unpin — Remove from the top.
  • Hide — Hide the post from public view (admin only).
  • Unhide — Restore visibility.
⚠️ Note: Deleting a post also removes all likes, comments, and reposts associated with it.

Reporting Posts
Report inappropriate content to moderators.
▼

How to report a post

  1. On any post, tap the 🚩 Report button (if visible).
  2. Provide a reason for the report in the text field.
  3. Tap "Submit Report".

The report will be sent to SPIKE admins for review. If the post violates guidelines, it will be removed.

💡 Tip: You can also report users from their profile.

Chat Room
Real-time messaging with typing indicators, read receipts, and reactions.
▼

Entering a Chat

Tap a chat from the Chat List to open the room.

Features in a chat room

  • Typing indicator — see when the other person is typing.
  • Read receipts — single tick (✓) delivered, double tick (✓✓) read.
  • Reactions — long press or tap the reaction button to add an emoji (❤️, 😂, 😮, 😢, 😡).
  • Message actions — tap ⋮ on a message to copy, edit (own messages within 5 min), delete, or reply.
  • Voice & Video calls — tap the 📞 or 📹 buttons to start a call.
  • Media sharing — attach images or record voice notes.
💡 Tip: Use the reply feature to respond to a specific message, keeping the conversation organized.
📞
Voice & Video Calls
Make encrypted voice and video calls directly from the chat room.
▼

Starting a call

  1. Open a chat room.
  2. Tap the 📞 (Voice) or 📹 (Video) button in the header.
  3. Wait for the other person to accept.

During a call

  • Mute — toggle your microphone (M key).
  • Speaker — toggle speakerphone (S key for voice, speaker button for video).
  • Camera (video) — turn your camera on/off (V key).
  • Flip camera (video) — switch between front and rear (F key).
  • End call — tap the red button or press Escape.
💡 Tip: Calls are end-to-end encrypted. Ensure you have a stable internet connection for best quality.
⚠️ Note: Both parties must grant microphone (and camera for video) permissions.
🎤
Voice Notes
Record voice notes up to 60 seconds. Visualizer shows audio levels.
▼

How to send a voice note

  1. In a chat, tap the 🎤 microphone button next to the input field.
  2. Press and hold the record button to start recording (or tap to toggle in some interfaces).
  3. Speak clearly. The waveform visualizer shows audio levels.
  4. Release or tap again to stop recording.
  5. Review the recording and tap Send.
💡 Tip: Voice notes are stored in the cloud and can be played back inline. They are automatically transcribed in some versions.
⚠️ Note: Voice notes have a maximum length of 60 seconds.
👁️
Read Receipts
Single check means delivered; double check in blue means read.
▼

Read receipts help you know if your message has been seen.

  • ✓ (grey) — message sent and delivered to the recipient's device.
  • ✓✓ (grey) — message delivered and the recipient has read it.
  • ✓✓ (blue) — message read, and the recipient is online or has seen it recently.
💡 Tip: You can disable read receipts in Settings → Privacy if you prefer not to show when you've read messages.

  • Profile Visibility — Public (everyone), Friends (only friends), Private (only you).
  • SPIKE ID Search — Everyone, Friends, No One (controls who can find you by ID).
  • Online Status — Show/hide your online status to others.
  • Friend Requests — Everyone, Friends, None (who can send you requests).
💡 Tip: Set "SPIKE ID Search" to "No One" for maximum privacy.
🔇
Content Filters
Mute words and block users to control what you see.
▼

Muted Words

Add words to mute — posts containing these words will be hidden from your feed.

  1. In Settings → Content, find "Muted Words".
  2. Type a word and tap "Add".
  3. To remove, click the ✕ on the tag.

Blocked Users

Block a user by UID (found on their profile). Blocked users' content is hidden.

💡 Tip: Blocking a user also prevents them from contacting you.
📂
Data & Storage
Export data, reset settings, and clear cache.
▼
  • Export Data — Download a JSON file containing all your SPIKE data (settings, posts, messages, etc.).
  • Reset Settings — Restore all settings to their default values (does not delete account data).
  • Clear Cache — Free up storage by clearing cached content (posts, avatars, etc.). You remain logged in.
💡 Tip: Export your data regularly for backup purposes.

Account Management
Change password, update email, or delete your account permanently.
▼

Change Password

In Settings → Account, tap "Change Password". You'll be guided through the process.

Update Email

Tap "Email Address" to update your registered email.

Delete Account

Tap "Delete Account" and confirm. This action is irreversible and deletes all your data.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All posts, messages, and coins will be lost.

Announcement System
Create a scrolling ticker announcement visible on the feed.
▼

Accessing the Announcement page

Admin users can go to the Announcement page from the admin panel.

Creating an announcement

  1. Write your announcement text in the textarea.
  2. Tap "Save Announcement".
  3. Toggle the visibility switch to show/hide it on the feed.

Preview

The announcement scrolls across the feed in a ticker style. It syncs in real-time with Firebase.

💡 Tip: Keep announcements short for the best scrolling effect.

Admin Panel
Full control over reports, user management, and maintenance.
▼

The Admin Panel (administrator.html) gives admins access to:

  • Reports — review user reports on posts and users.
  • User Management — view user details, suspend accounts.
  • Announcements — create and manage announcements.
  • Maintenance Mode — enable/disable maintenance mode for the whole app.
💡 Tip: Use maintenance mode during updates or downtime.
⏳
Maintenance Mode
Enable maintenance mode to show a maintenance page to all users.
▼

When enabled, non-admin users are redirected to a maintenance page with a countdown timer.

How to enable

In the Admin Panel, toggle the "Maintenance Mode" switch. Set an estimated downtime (ETA).

Admins can still access the app to manage settings during maintenance.

💡 Tip: Use maintenance mode to perform updates without disrupting users.
