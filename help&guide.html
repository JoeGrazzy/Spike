SPIKE Help & Guide

Everything you need to know about using SPIKE — from getting started to advanced features.

📋 Table of Contents
🚀 Getting Started ▼
📱 What is SPIKE?
SPIKE is a real‑time social platform where you can connect with friends, share posts, chat, join communities, and build your network. Everything is designed for speed and simplicity.
📝 Creating Your Account
Sign up using your email address and a secure password. You'll also need to verify your phone number with a 6‑digit OTP code.
1. Go to the signup page
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number
3. Verify your phone number with the OTP code
4. Create a password and submit
5. Check your email for verification link
🆔 Your SPIKE ID
Every user gets a permanent SPIKE ID (e.g., SP-123456) when they sign up. This ID is unique to you and can be used to find and connect with others.
🔑 Signing In
Use your email and password to sign in. You can also enable Remember Me to stay logged in across sessions.
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Check "Remember Me" to stay signed in
4. Click Sign In
🔐 Forgot Password?
If you forget your password, click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link.
👤 Profile & Identity ▼
📸 Setting Up Your Profile
Your profile is your identity on SPIKE. Complete it to help others find and connect with you.
1. Go to your Profile page
2. Tap the avatar to upload a photo
3. Tap the "Data" button to view and edit all your profile fields
4. Click ✏️ Edit Profile to make changes
📋 Profile Fields
You can fill in various fields to share more about yourself:
• Basic: Name, Username, Bio, Mood
• Personal: Location, DOB, Gender, Pronouns, Relationship
• Professional: Occupation, Company, Education, Industry
• Social: Website, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, GitHub, LinkedIn
• Interests & Skills: Add multiple tags to showcase your hobbies and talents
🔒 Privacy Controls
Each profile field has a privacy setting — Public or Private.
• Public: Visible to everyone on SPIKE
• Private: Only visible to you
• Toggle visibility in the Profile page
🆔 Finding Users by SPIKE ID
You can find other users by their SPIKE ID. Go to Settings → Find Users and enter the SPIKE ID to search.
✅ Verification Badge ▼
⭐ What is the Verification Badge?
The Verified Badge is a gold checkmark that appears on your profile. It shows that you are a trusted, genuine member of the SPIKE community.
📊 Requirements
To unlock verification, you must meet all three requirements:
📅 1 Year Active 👥 500 Friends 📝 1,000 Posts
✅ 292/365 days ✅ 450/500 friends ⏳ 600/1,000 posts
You can track your progress on your Profile page under the verification section.
💰 How to Apply
Once you meet all requirements:
1. Go to Get Verified
2. Select your preferred plan (One-Time, Monthly, or Yearly)
3. Make a bank transfer to the provided account
4. Upload your payment receipt
5. Submit for admin approval
Processing time: 10-30 minutes
❓ What happens after applying?
Your request enters Pending Review status. An admin will review your application and approve or reject it.
✅ Approved: You receive the Verified Badge permanently
❌ Rejected: You can reapply after a 5-minute cooldown
📝 Posts & Content ▼
✍️ Creating a Post
Share your thoughts with the SPIKE community.
1. Go to the Feed page
2. Type your message in the "What's happening?" box
3. Attach an 📷 Image or 🎥 Video (optional)
4. Click Post
❤️ Interacting with Posts
You can interact with posts in multiple ways:
❤️ Like: Click the heart icon
😂😮😢😡 React: Click the ⋯ button to open reactions
💬 Comment: Click the comment icon and type your reply
🔁 Repost: Share the post to your own feed
🔗 Share: Send the post to a friend via chat
🔧 Managing Your Posts
As the author, you can:
✏️ Edit: Click the ⋮ menu and select Edit
📌 Pin: Pin the post to the top of your feed (Elite or Mod)
🔒 Hide: Hide the post from public view
🗑️ Delete: Permanently remove the post
🔗 Link Previews
When you paste a link into the post composer, SPIKE automatically generates a rich preview with the title, description, and image.
🤝 Connections ▼
👥 Friends System
Connect with other SPIKE users by becoming friends.
📩 Sending a Friend Request
1. Go to Friends
2. Search for a user by name
3. Click Add Friend
4. Wait for them to accept
📬 Managing Friend Requests
Go to Friends → 📩 Requests tab to:
✅ Accept: Start chatting and sharing
❌ Decline: Reject the request
🚫 Blocking Users
Blocking a user prevents them from seeing your profile, sending messages, or interacting with you.
1. Go to Friends
2. Find the user and click 🚫 Block
3. Confirm the action
Blocked users appear in the 🚫 Blocked tab. You can unblock them anytime.
💬 Messaging & Calls ▼
💬 Chatting with Friends
Start a conversation with any friend.
1. Go to Messages
2. Click ✚ New Chat
3. Select a friend from the list
4. Start typing and send!
📎 Sending Media in Chat
You can send more than just text messages:
🖼️ Images: Tap the 📎 button and select Photo
🎤 Voice Notes: Tap the 📎 button and select Voice
📨 Shared Posts: Share posts directly from the feed
📞 Voice & Video Calls
Make real-time voice and video calls with your friends.
1. Open a chat with a friend
2. Tap the 📞 Call button
3. Choose Voice or Video
4. Wait for them to answer
Calls are end-to-end encrypted for your privacy.
👁️ Read Receipts & Typing Indicators
See when your messages are read (✓ delivered, ✓✓ read) and when the other person is typing (... typing).
🗑️ Deleting a Chat
Permanently delete an entire conversation by clicking the 🗑️ button in the chat header.
🏠 Communities ▼
🌍 What are Communities?
Communities are spaces where people with shared interests can connect, share posts, and have discussions. They are the heart of SPIKE's social experience.
➕ Joining a Community
1. Go to Communities
2. Browse the list of communities
3. For 🌍 Public: Click ➕ Join
4. For 🔒 Private: Click 🔒 Request to Join and wait for admin approval
👑 Community Roles
👑 Creator: The founder of the community
🛡️ Admin: Manages members and approves join requests
👤 Member: Can post and interact
📝 Posting in a Community
1. Open the community
2. Type your post in the "What's on your mind?" box
3. Click 📤 Post
🛠️ Community Admin Features
Admins can:
📋 Approve or reject join requests
👑 Promote or demote admins
🚫 Remove or suspend members
✏️ Edit community details (name, description, privacy)
🗑️ Delete the community
📌 Pin important posts
🚩 Manage reported posts
Admin panel is accessible via the 🛡️ Admin button in the community detail page.
🚪 Leaving a Community
Click the Leave button in the community detail page. You will lose access to all community content.
🪙 Coins & Shop ▼
🪙 What are SPIKE Coins?
SPIKE Coins are the platform's virtual currency. You can use them to:
• Upgrade to VIP (200 coins), Premium (500 coins), or Elite (1,000 coins)
• Send gifts to other users
• Enter rooms (entry fee)
• Transfer to other users via SP:ID/amount
🏪 The Shop
Visit the Shop to view available coin packages. Prices range from $0.09 (10 coins) to $39.99 (10,000 coins).
• Package selection: Choose the amount you need
• Contact admin: Use the 📞 button in any room to message the admin on WhatsApp
• Provide: Your name, SPIKE ID, and selected package
• Payment: Admin will send you payment details
• Delivery: Coins are added within 10-30 minutes
📡 Signals
You can set a Signal on your profile to indicate how many coins you are willing to receive from others. This helps others know that you are open to receiving coin transfers.
1. Go to Shop
2. Set your desired coin amount using the +/− buttons or presets
3. Click Set Signal
4. Others will see your signal on your profile
↗️ Sending Coins
You can send coins to any SPIKE user using the SP:ID/amount command in any room chat.
1. Type SP:USER_SPIKE_ID/AMOUNT (e.g., SP:123456/50)
2. Send the message
3. The coins are instantly deducted from your balance and added to the recipient
You can also send coins by tapping the Send Coins button on a user's profile (if they have a signal set).
📊 Transaction History
All your coin transactions (purchases, sends, and receives) are stored in your 📜 Transaction History, visible in the Shop page.
💎 Tier Benefits
Upgrading to higher tiers unlocks exclusive features:
👑 VIP (200 🪙): Stickers, VIP Crown, 2x daily bonus
⭐ Premium (500 🪙): Voice Notes, 2.5x daily bonus
🔥 Elite (1,000 🪙): Image Sharing, Polls, Custom Colour, Pin Messages, 4x daily bonus
All tier features are visible in the ⭐ ELITE panel inside any room.
⚙️ Settings & Privacy ▼
🎨 Appearance
Customize how SPIKE looks:
🌙 Dark Mode: Toggle between dark and light theme
🎨 Accent Color: Choose from 5 colors (Green, Blue, Purple, Red, Yellow)
🔒 Privacy Settings
👀 Profile Visibility: Public, Friends, or Private
🔍 SPIKE ID Search: Everyone, Friends, or No One
📱 Online Status: Show when you're active
👤 Friend Requests: Everyone, Friends, or None
🔔 Notification Preferences
Control which notifications you receive:
❤️ Likes & Reactions
💬 Comments & Replies
👥 Friend Requests
🔔 Push Notifications
🤫 Muted Words
Add words or phrases to automatically hide posts containing them. This helps you filter out unwanted content.
1. Go to Settings → Content
2. Type a word in the Muted Words section
3. Click Add
💾 Saved Posts
Save posts you want to revisit later. Your saved posts are stored securely and accessible from Settings.
🛡️ Safety & Security ▼
🚫 Blocking Users
If someone is bothering you, you can block them. Blocked users cannot:
• See your profile or posts
• Send you messages
• Find you in search
• Send friend requests
You can unblock users anytime from the 🚫 Blocked tab in Friends.
🚩 Reporting Content
If you see inappropriate content, you can report it for admin review.
1. Click the ⋮ menu on the post
2. Select 🚩 Report
3. Provide a reason
4. Submit
Admins review all reports and take appropriate action.
⛔ Account Suspension
If you violate community guidelines, your account may be suspended by an admin. A suspended account cannot:
• Post content
• Send messages
• Join communities
• Interact with others
If you believe this is a mistake, contact support for assistance.
🛠️ Admin Guide ▼
📢 Announcements
Admins can create scrolling announcements that appear at the top of the feed.
1. Go to Admin Dashboard
2. Navigate to Announcements
3. Type your announcement text
4. Set an expiry date (optional)
5. Click 💾 Save
🚩 Reports Management
Admins can view and manage reported posts.
1. Go to Admin Dashboard → Reports
2. Review pending reports
3. ✓ Mark Resolved or 🗑️ Delete Post
🏠 Community Management
Admins can manage all communities, including:
📋 Approving join requests
👑 Promoting/demoting admins
🚫 Removing/suspending members
✏️ Editing community details
🗑️ Deleting communities
🔧 Maintenance Mode
Admins can put the platform into maintenance mode. During maintenance, users see a "Under Maintenance" page.
🪙 Coin Management
Admins can add or remove coins from any user's balance via the Admin Dashboard.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions ▼
🔑 I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
Click "Forgot password?" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions in the reset email.
📧 I didn't receive the verification email. What should I do?
Check your spam folder. If it's not there, you can request a new verification email from the Profile page.
🆔 Can I change my SPIKE ID?
No. Your SPIKE ID is permanent and cannot be changed. It's your unique identifier on the platform.
📝 Can I edit a post after posting?
Yes! Click the ⋮ menu on your post and select ✏️ Edit.
🔒 How do I make my profile private?
Go to Settings → Privacy and set "Profile Visibility" to Private.
🌙 How do I enable Dark Mode?
Go to Settings → Appearance and toggle the 🌙 Dark Mode switch.
✅ How long does verification take?
After you meet the requirements and submit your application, verification typically takes 10-30 minutes for admin review.
👥 How do I unfriend someone?
Go to Friends, find the user, and click Unfriend.
📱 Is SPIKE available on mobile?
SPIKE is a fully responsive web app that works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile devices.
🏠 What's the difference between Communities and Groups?
SPIKE uses a unified Communities system. Communities are social spaces for shared interests with full features including posts, comments, likes, and admin controls. The old "Groups" system has been merged into Communities for a better experience.
🪙 How do I buy coins?
Visit the Shop, select your package, and click the 📞 button in any room to contact the admin on WhatsApp. Provide your name, SPIKE ID, and desired package. The admin will guide you through payment and coin delivery.
📡 What is a signal?
A signal shows other users that you are open to receiving coins. You set a desired amount in the Shop page, and it appears on your profile. Others can send you coins directly via the profile modal.
💎 What are the tier benefits?
See the Coins & Shop section above for a full breakdown of VIP, Premium, and Elite benefits.
