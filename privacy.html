Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how SPIKE collects, uses, and protects your information.

📅 Last Updated: March 2026
Overview

📋 Our Commitment

SPIKE is built with privacy at its core. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we handle it. This policy applies to all SPIKE services, including the mobile app, web platform, and any related services.

By using SPIKE, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We regularly review our practices to ensure we meet the highest privacy standards.

Data We Collect

📊 What We Collect

To provide you with the best experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information — Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture you provide during registration.
  • Content You Create — Posts, messages, comments, reactions, and any other content you share on SPIKE.
  • Usage Data — How you interact with our platform, including features used, time spent, and navigation patterns.
  • Device Information — Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address for analytics and security.
  • Location Data — Approximate location based on IP address (we do not collect precise GPS data without consent).

We never collect sensitive information like financial data, government IDs, or biometric data unless explicitly required by law.

Data Usage

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

Your data helps us deliver, improve, and secure SPIKE. Here's how we use it:

  • Provide Services — To operate and maintain your account, deliver messages, and show your feed.
  • Personalize Experience — To tailor content, suggestions, and notifications to your interests.
  • Security & Safety — To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Analytics & Improvement — To understand usage patterns and improve platform performance.
  • Communication — To send service updates, security alerts, and occasional product news.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Period.

Data Sharing

🤝 When We Share Data

We share your data only in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers — Trusted partners who help us operate SPIKE (e.g., Cloudinary for media hosting, Firebase for authentication). They are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Compliance — When required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Business Transfers — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred.
  • With Your Consent — When you explicitly allow us to share specific information.

🔒 All data transmitted between you and SPIKE is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.

Cookies

🍪 Cookies & Tracking

SPIKE uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, remember your preferences, and analyze usage. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

Your Rights

⚖️ Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights under applicable law (GDPR, CCPA, etc.):

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Data Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent — Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Object to Processing — Object to certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Security

🛡️ How We Protect Your Data

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Encryption — All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access Control — Strict role-based access to personal data.
  • Regular Audits — Security reviews and vulnerability assessments.
  • Data Minimization — We only collect what we need.
  • Secure Development — Privacy-first approach in our development lifecycle.
Children

🧒 Children's Privacy

SPIKE is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it.

For users in the EU, the minimum age is 16 unless local law provides otherwise.
