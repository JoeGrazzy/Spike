📚 Frequently Asked Questions 19 articles
How do I create a SPIKE account? ▼
Creating a SPIKE account is easy! Visit the home page and click "Get Started." Enter your name, email, phone number, and create a secure password. You'll receive a verification code via SMS and a confirmation email to activate your account.
How do I reset my password? ▼
On the sign-in page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. We'll send you a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder.
How do I delete my account? ▼
You can delete your account anytime from your profile settings. Scroll to the bottom of the settings page and click "Delete Account." You'll be asked to confirm your decision. Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Your data will be removed within 30 days in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
Is my data secure on SPIKE? ▼
Yes! We take security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We use industry-standard security practices, including regular audits and vulnerability assessments. We never share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
How do I start a chat with someone? ▼
To start a chat, go to the Messages page and click the "New Chat" button. You can search for users by name or SPIKE ID. Select the person you want to chat with and start typing! You can also start a chat from a user's profile by clicking the message icon.
Can I make voice or video calls on SPIKE? ▼
Yes! SPIKE supports both voice and video calls. In any chat, tap the phone icon for voice calls or the camera icon for video calls. You'll need to grant microphone and camera permissions. Calls are encrypted and peer-to-peer for the best quality.
How do I send a voice message? ▼
In any chat, tap the attachment icon (📎) and select "Voice." Hold the record button to start speaking. Release to send. You can also cancel by swiping left. Voice messages are stored securely and can be played back anytime.
How do I share a post on my feed? ▼
To share a post, go to the Feed page and tap the "Create Post" button. Add text, images, or emojis to express yourself. You can also tag friends and add location. Once you're happy, tap "Share" to publish to your followers.
Can I edit or delete my post? ▼
Yes! On your post, tap the three-dot menu (⋯) and select "Edit" to make changes, or "Delete" to remove the post. Deleted posts are permanently removed from the feed. You can also manage your posts from your profile page.
Who can see my profile and posts? ▼
You control your privacy settings. You can choose between Public (everyone), Friends Only, or Private. In Private mode, only approved followers can see your content. You can also block users and mute words to control your experience.
The app is loading slowly. What should I do? ▼
Try these steps to improve performance:

• Check your internet connection
• Close and reopen the app
• Clear your browser cache
• Update to the latest version
• Disable any VPN or proxy

If the issue persists, contact our support team.
Is SPIKE free to use? ▼
SPIKE is completely free to use! All core features — including messaging, voice/video calls, feed posts, and group chats — are available at no cost. We may introduce premium features in the future, but the basics will always be free.
How do I change my display name or profile picture? ▼
Go to your Profile page and tap the edit icon (✎) next to your name or avatar. You can update your name, bio, and upload a new profile picture. Changes are saved instantly and visible to other users immediately.
Can I pin chats to the top? ▼
Yes! In your chat list, you'll see a pin icon (📌) on each chat. Tap it to pin the chat to the top of your list. Pinned chats stay at the top until you unpin them. This is great for keeping important conversations accessible.
How do I report a bug or issue? ▼
We appreciate your help in making SPIKE better! To report a bug, use the contact form below or email us at support@spike.mediachat.workers.dev. Please include details about the issue, steps to reproduce it, and your device/browser information.
How do I block or report a user? ▼
On a user's profile, tap the three-dot menu (⋯) and select "Block" or "Report." Blocked users can no longer message you or see your content. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, and action will be taken if the user violates our Terms of Service.
What is a SPIKE ID and how do I find mine? ▼
Your SPIKE ID is a unique 6-digit identifier assigned to your account. You can find it in your profile settings or in the "Find by SPIKE ID" section. Share your ID with friends so they can find and add you easily. You can also use it to search for other users.
Are my messages end-to-end encrypted? ▼
Yes! All messages on SPIKE are encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL. While we don't currently use end-to-end encryption for every message, we are working on implementing it in a future update. Your conversations are always private and protected.
How do I save a post to view later? ▼
On any post, tap the save icon (🔖) to bookmark it. Saved posts can be accessed from the "Saved Posts" section in your settings. This is a great way to keep track of content you want to revisit.
